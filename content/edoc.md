HIDDEN LAYERS is proposed as a workshop co-located with **EDOC/CBI 2026**, taking place on **September 15–18, 2026** at the **University of Twente**.

The workshop complements the EDOC community by focusing on security-oriented enterprise concerns at the intersection of:

- Enterprise risk and threat analysis
- AI-driven forensic methods
- Image security and hidden malicious content

For conference-level information, please refer to official EDOC/CBI channels.
