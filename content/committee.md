The committee will include experts from academia and industry in enterprise risk, AI forensics, cybersecurity, and image security.

## Workshop Co-Chairs

- **Dipti K. Sarmah** is a Docent at the University of Twente, The Netherlands, and a member of the Semantics, Cybersecurity & Services (SCS) group. Her research focuses on image steganography and steganalysis, cryptography-steganography fusion, secure authentication, phishing and social engineering, and applied cybersecurity, with a strong emphasis on real-world and educational contexts. She is actively involved in teaching and supervising Bachelor’s and Master’s research projects. Dipti holds a Ph.D. in Information Security and has published her work in reputed venues such as Computers & Security, Information Sciences, International Journal of Information Security, and Security & Privacy, as well as conferences including IEEE EuroS&P and CSCML. Dipti also served as Poster Chair of IEEE
EuroS&P 2024.

- **I Putu Arya Dharmaadi** is a PhD researcher in the Information Systems Research Group at the University of Groningen, The Netherlands. His research focuses on fuzzing, automated testing, and web security, with an emphasis on identifying and mitigating vulnerabilities in complex software systems. His recent work on web fuzzing was published in the International Journal of Information Security. He has served as a reviewer for venues including ACM Computing Surveys, IEEE Transactions on Software Engineering, the Information Security Journal: A Global Perspective (Taylor & Francis), and the 30th ACM Symposium on Access Control Models and Technologies.
 
- **Marianne Junger** received the Ph.D. degree in law from the Free University of Amsterdam, Amsterdam, the Netherlands, in 1990. She is the Emeritus Professor of Cyber Security and Business Continuity with the University of Twente, Enschede, the Netherlands. Her research investigates the human factors of fraud and cybercrime. More specifically, she investigates online victimisation, disclosure, and privacy issues. She founded the Crime Science journal together with Pieter Hartel and was an Associate Editor for 6 years. Her research was sponsored by, among others, the Dutch Police, NWO, ZonMw (for health research), and the European Union.

- **Claudia Negri Ribalta** earned her PhD at the Université Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne where she focused on the development of a socio-technical data protection framework for requirements with divergent mental models. She is a MSCA COFUND fellow and research associate at the Interdisciplinary Research Group in Socio-technical Cybersecurity (IRiSC) at the SnT, and the Institute of Advance Studies (IAS), both at the University of Luxembourg. Currently, she is also with the Technical University of Valencia. Claudia’s expertise lies in data protection, socio-technical cyber security, requirements engineering, user-centered design and hypothesis building. Her main area of interest is new technologies' requirements for data protection, cybersecurity, and socio-technical challenges. Her research, in particular, takes an interdisciplinary and user-centered point of view, taking special consideration of regulations. She is president and co-founder of the NGO OptIA, which focuses on transparent and inclusive algorithms, and their impacts in public policy.
