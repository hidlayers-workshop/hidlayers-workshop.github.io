The committees are experts from academia and industry in enterprise risk, AI forensics, cybersecurity, and image security.

## Workshop Co-Chairs

- **Dipti K. Sarmah** – Docent at the University of Twente, The Netherlands. Research on image steganography, cryptography-steganography fusion, secure authentication, phishing, social engineering, and applied cybersecurity.  
- **I Putu Arya Dharmaadi** – PhD researcher, University of Groningen, The Netherlands. Focus on fuzzing, automated testing, and web security.  
- **Marianne Junger** – Emeritus Professor, University of Twente. Research on human factors of fraud and cybercrime.  
- **Claudia Negri Ribalta** – Research associate, Interdisciplinary Research Group in Socio-technical Cybersecurity, Luxembourg. Focus on data protection and socio-technical cybersecurity.
